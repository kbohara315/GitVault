# Approve a qualified platform release

## Question

After native tests and recovery drills exist, which OS/architecture/filesystem combinations are supported, who supplies signing credentials, and may the resulting artifacts be published?

Evidence must distinguish native runtime qualification from cross-compilation. Supply credentials only through a private secret mechanism, not chat or source. This human decision remains open; task completion or research cannot approve release, create signing material, or invent a supported-platform claim.
